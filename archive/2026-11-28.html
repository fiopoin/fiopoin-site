Stocks and your savings

US stocks post their best week since April 2025 while bond yields climb.

Medium stakes for savers

Happened

In Friday's shortened session the S&P 500 rose 1.60% to 7,297.49, the Dow 591.99 points, or 1.18%, to 50,774.94 and the Nasdaq 2.31% to 24,129.50, N Globe reports. For the week the S&P gained 5.6%, the Nasdaq 6.7% and the Dow 5.5%. All 11 sectors rose, technology led at 2.80%, and the VIX fear gauge fell 15.84% to 23.81, CappNotes reports.

Happening

The 10 year Treasury yield rose 9 basis points to 4.94%, the 2 year to 4.69% and the 30 year to 5.33%. The dollar index rose 0.36% to 104.04. CME FedWatch showed market odds of 97.4% for a quarter point hike at the Fed's December 8 to 9 meeting, CappNotes says. Next week brings ISM manufacturing and JOLTS job openings on Tuesday, December 1.

Could happen

A strong rebound week is meeting rising yields and a likely Fed hike, a mix that can limit further stock gains into December.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • After a 5.6% week, check whether stocks now make up more of your savings than you planned, and rebalance if so.
  • Shopping for a mortgage or car loan? Ask for a rate lock; the 10 year yield, which steers mortgage rates, rose to 4.94%.
  • Holding cash? Compare high yield savings and certificate rates, which tend to rise after Fed hikes.

Oil and the Gulf

Iran says it is negotiating with the US through Qatar and Pakistan as OPEC+ meets Sunday.

High stakes for fuel prices

Happened

Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said on November 26 that Tehran is negotiating with the US through Qatari and Pakistani mediators and that Iran has demands that "must be met, so the Strait of Hormuz can be opened," Political.org reports. Deals and companies

Dangote's refinery is set to start trading in Lagos on Tuesday after a $2.08 billion share sale.

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Dangote Petroleum Refinery will begin trading on the Nigerian Exchange on Tuesday, December 1, a week earlier than planned, Billionaires.Africa reported on November 27, citing Bloomberg. The offer raised $2.08 billion at ₦525 a share, 30% above its base target, with bids of about ₦5.85 trillion ($4.3 billion). The listing values the refinery at about ₦65.2 trillion ($47.8 billion).

Happening

Aliko Dangote's stake falls from about 87.3% to roughly 84%. Intellinews said in September the sale was expected to be Africa's biggest ever. In the US, CrowdStrike agreed on November 23 to buy Zscaler in an all stock deal worth about $43.9 billion, a premium of roughly 34%, SecurityWeek reports; Infosecurity Magazine calls it the largest cybersecurity acquisition ever.

Could happen

The first days of trading will test how much demand there is for a large Nigerian listing; new listings often swing sharply at first.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

Argentina's Congress bars the central bank from printing money to fund the government.

Argentina's Chamber of Deputies gave final approval on November 26 to a law that limits central bank financing of the government and makes protecting the currency's value its main mission, by 141 to zero with 99 abstentions, Al Jazeera reports. Recycling and chemistry

Chemists turn hard to recycle PVC plastic into high performance lubricating oil.

A Virginia Tech team led by Guoliang Liu converted PVC into polyalphaolefin, a synthetic lubricant base, by heating it with aluminum trichloride and alpha olefins to 158°F for three hours, ScienceDaily reported on November 27. The work, with Texas A&M and Caltech, is published in Nature. Scaling beyond the lab remains the next step.

For you: Not yet a product; for now, check local rules, since PVC is rarely accepted in curbside recycling.

ScienceDaily, Virginia Tech, Nov 27
