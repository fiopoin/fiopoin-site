Gas and Thanksgiving travel

Gas fell 18 cents in a week to $3.72. It may fall further before Thanksgiving.

Medium stakes for drivers

Happened

The AAA national average was $3.72 on November 19, down from $3.91 a week earlier and $4.30 a month ago. It is still about 60 cents above a year ago. Prices are under $4 in 40 states.

Happening

US crude has slid into the low $80s and stockpiles rose 3.4 million barrels. AAA expects nearly 73 million people to take Thanksgiving road trips next week and says the average could drop another 15 cents by Thursday, avoiding a holiday record. The record is $3.58, set on Thanksgiving 2022.

Could happen

Falling crude points to cheaper gas into the holiday. A flare up in the Gulf is the main risk to that.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • Driving for Thanksgiving? Use a price app to compare stations. The gap within a few miles is often bigger than the weekly drop.
  • California averages $5.51 and Texas $3.21. If your route crosses state lines, fill up in the cheaper state.
  • Put the savings toward variable rate debt. The Fed still signals more rate hikes, and card rates follow.
Watch: AAA's update before Thanksgiving, November 26.AAA, MLive via Yahoo Finance
Iran war and oil

High stakes for prices

Brent traded at $92.22 in Asia on Friday, on course for a weekly fall of about 3.5%, its third in a row, as Gulf crude shipments recover. Houthi strikes damaged the PIF Tower in Riyadh's financial district, with no injuries reported. Iran's Revolutionary Guards say Hormuz will not fully reopen until the US meets their conditions.

AI stocks

Anthropic jumped 34% at the open, then gave back half. It is lower again before Friday's bell.

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Anthropic's first trade on the Nasdaq was $111.50, 34% above its $83 IPO price, with 108 million shares changing hands. It closed Thursday at $97.17, up 17.07%, on a day when the Nasdaq fell 2.25%.

Happening

ANT traded at $90.99 at 4:01 a.m. Eastern Friday, down 6.36% from Thursday's close but still above $83. Nvidia stopped shipping advanced AI chips to China, and Colette Kress cited $2.4 billion in quarterly China sales.

Could happen

A small public float means big daily swings in both directions. Expect the price to stay volatile while the broader market looks for a floor.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact. Not investment advice.

Gold and Bitcoin

Gold and Bitcoin fell with stocks. Bitcoin is near $60,000 as ETF money leaves.

Medium stakes for savers

Happened

Gold futures settled down 1.50% at $3,685.50 on Thursday. Bitcoin fell 2.15% to $61,635.18 by early evening. US spot Bitcoin ETFs lost more than $300 million on Wednesday, taking 2026 net outflows past $2.5 billion.

Happening

Gold was heading for a weekly drop of about 9% on Friday, its steepest in eight months, as the dollar sits near a 19 month high and the Fed signals more rate hikes.

Could happen

When rates rise and the dollar is strong, assets that pay no interest tend to struggle. That pressure lasts as long as the Fed keeps hiking.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact. Not investment advice.

What it means for you

  • Gold and crypto did not cushion this selloff. Do not count on them as your only safety net.
  • For money you need within a year, insured savings and Treasury bills now pay near the Fed rate.
  • Keep any crypto to a share of savings you could lose without changing your plans.

Around the world

One story per region, weighted toward where most people live. Each ends with what it means for you.

China and East Asia

China's shoppers pulled back hard in October.

Retail sales grew just 0.4% from a year earlier, down from 1.1% in September, and car sales fell 27.1%. Consumer prices rose 0.7%. Exports, by contrast, jumped 35.2% to $412.33 billion.

For you: Weak demand at home pushes Chinese factories to sell abroad. Expect plenty of low priced Chinese goods, and more trade friction over them.

Covers China, Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Southeast Asia. China Briefing

India and South Asia

The rupee hits a seven month high on foreign buying.

The rupee settled at 93.30 per dollar on Thursday, its fourth straight gain. Foreign investors have put more than ₹88,000 crore into Indian stocks in November, the fastest monthly pace since November 2020.

For you: Sending dollars to India? Each dollar buys a little less than last week. Paying Indian suppliers in rupees costs a bit more.

Covers India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Nepal. Business Standard

Europe

UK inflation edges up to 3.6%.

Britain's statistics office said inflation rose to 3.6% in October from 3.5%, a touch below the 3.7% forecast. UK and EU leaders hold a summit in Brussels on Friday.

For you: Planning a UK trip or paying UK bills? Prices are still rising well above the Bank of England's 2% goal, which keeps UK rates high.

Covers European Union, United Kingdom, Ukraine, Russia. The Rio Times

Middle East

Houthi strikes damage the PIF Tower in Riyadh.

Saudi Civil Defence said the attack damaged the headquarters of the kingdom's sovereign wealth fund in the King Abdullah Financial District. No injuries were reported. The UAE, Qatar, Kuwait and the GCC condemned it.

For you: Traveling to or through Saudi Arabia? Check airline and embassy alerts before you go.

Covers Gulf states, Iran, Israel, Iraq, Yemen. Gulf News

Africa

Nigeria's inflation hits a six year low, but cooking gas is up.

Inflation fell to 14.06% in October. Cooking gas now costs ₦1,600 to ₦1,800 per kg in several cities, against about ₦1,400 a month ago, and some households are turning back to charcoal. The central bank meets November 23 to 24 with its rate at 23%.

For you: Sending money to Nigeria? The naira was ₦1,363 per dollar on November 18. Household fuel is eating into what remittances buy.

Covers Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Egypt. The Nigeria Brief

South America

Brazil's real and Argentina's peso slide as Wall Street sells off.

The real fell to 5.93 per dollar, its weakest since April 2025. Argentina's peso weakened to 1,565 per dollar, its weakest close in at least 12 months, and the Merval fell 0.96%.

For you: A trip to Brazil or Argentina is getting cheaper in dollars. Locals there pay more for imported goods.

Covers Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru. The Rio Times, The Rio Times, Argentina

Front Office Sports reports the league was backed by more than $5 billion from the Public Investment Fund. With that funding ending, stars are leaving and events are being canceled.

Football, Brazil

Brazil's football federation suspends a regional cup as betting limits hit sponsors.

The CBF suspended the South-Southeast Cup for 2027 after betting restrictions disrupted sponsorship talks. It forecasts a R$420 million revenue shortfall next year. Separately, Mercado Libre renewed its CONMEBOL club sponsorship through 2030.

For you: Betting sponsors fund much of global sport. Rule changes on gambling can quickly shrink schedules and raise ticket prices.

Climate tech

A new material captures carbon even from humid exhaust.

Oregon State University chemists built BVR-X, a metal-organic framework that keeps water and CO2 apart in its pores. It captured CO2 from a humid gas with only 4% CO2 and kept working over dozens of cycles. A patent has been filed.

For you: Humidity is a big cost in carbon capture. Cheaper capture could matter for power and industrial bills over time.

AI and banking

Citi warns customers they may be liable for what AI agents do in their accounts.

Citi told customers they may be responsible for actions taken by third party AI tools they give account access to. TechCrunch reports more banks are taking the same line.

For you: Do not hand bank logins to AI assistants. Read your bank's terms before letting any tool move money.

AI and chips

OpenAI says its models helped redesign a chip.

OpenAI said its models worked on chip design with AMD. One redesign cut estimated chip area about 15% and a calculation from two clock cycles to one.

For you: Smaller chips can mean cheaper AI services. Watch for price cuts on AI tools you pay for.
