Gas, rates and Thanksgiving

Gas fell to $3.69, its lowest since March, as markets price in a December rate hike.

Medium stakes for households

Happened

The AAA national average was $3.69 on November 20, down from $3.90 on November 12 and $4.27 on October 22. Only eight states are above $4. North Carolina is cheapest at $3.04 and Hawaii highest at $5.59.

Happening

AAA says the average could fall another 15 cents by Thanksgiving, which would avoid a holiday record ($3.58 in 2022). At the same time CME FedWatch puts a 99.3% chance on a quarter point Fed hike at the December 8 to 9 meeting. Those are market odds, not a decision.

Could happen

Cheaper gas helps this week's budget, but a rate hike would push card and loan rates higher within weeks.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • Driving for Thanksgiving? Compare stations with a price app before you fill up.
  • Carrying a card balance? Pay down what you can before a December hike lifts variable rates.
  • Need a car loan or a fixed rate CD? Rates set before December 9 may be the better deal.
Watch: the Fed decision on December 9.AAA, MLive via Yahoo, USAGOLD
Iran war and oil

Iran sets seven conditions to reopen Hormuz. Oil still posts a third weekly loss.

High stakes for prices

Happened

Foreign Minister Araghchi said on Friday that seven conditions must be met before Hormuz reopens, including an end to the war and the lifting of sanctions and the US naval blockade. He ruled out sending Iran's 60% enriched uranium abroad. The US Treasury sanctioned four Iranians it called oil trustees.

Happening

Brent settled at $92.57 on Friday, up 0.64% on a reported tanker fire near Hormuz but down 3.41% for the week, its third weekly loss. WTI settled at $83.98, down 5.17% for the week. On Saturday Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed they shot down a US MQ-9 drone over the strait, a claim not independently confirmed.

Nvidia

Nvidia's 14% fall follows an SEC report, a CEO exit and a state subpoena.

High stakes for investors

Happened

Reuters reported Friday that SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia, after The Wall Street Journal said Tuesday that the SEC was investigating. The SEC declined to comment. Reuters noted that investigations are not evidence of wrongdoing. On Thursday California's attorney general served Nvidia an investigative subpoena over whether China obtained advanced chips.

Happening

Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO earlier this month and Colette Kress is interim CEO. Nvidia reported revenue up 103% to $115.8 billion last quarter and expects about $130 billion this quarter. The stock is down about 48% from its record close of $239.24 on October 6.

Could happen

Strong sales have not stopped the slide. Until the SEC review and the CEO search are clearer, expect big swings in Nvidia and in the funds that hold it.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact. Not investment advice.

What it means for you

  • Check how much of your savings sits in Nvidia through tech and S&P 500 funds. Many hold it as a top position.
  • Do not sell in a panic. Rebalance only if tech has grown past the share you planned.
  • Own single chip stocks? Decide your limit now and use limit orders, not market orders.
Watch: any statement from Nvidia or the SEC, and a permanent CEO pick.The Motley Fool, The Motley Fool, after the close
Gold, Bitcoin and bonds

Gold and Bitcoin fell with stocks. Treasury bonds were the shelter.

Medium stakes for savers

Happened

Spot gold fell 1.88% to $3,557.96 on Friday and touched $3,513.54, its lowest since September 2025. It lost about 8% this week. Bitcoin fell 6.01% to $58,186 by early evening, below $60,000 for the first time since July. Ether fell 9.03%.

Happening

Investors moved into Treasuries. The 10 year yield fell 12 basis points to 4.92% and the 2 year to 4.63%. The dollar index is near a 19 month high, about 103.8.

Could happen

With a rate hike priced in and the dollar strong, gold and crypto may stay under pressure. Falling yields help bond funds.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • Bond funds gained as yields fell. A mix of stocks and bonds is doing its job this week.
  • Holding Bitcoin? Size it as money you could see fall by half.
  • Buying gold as insurance? It can fall alongside stocks, as it did this week.
Watch: whether Bitcoin holds near its 2026 low.USAGOLD, FinanceFeeds, gold, The Motley Fool, crypto, CappNotes

China and East Asia

Foreign money into China's high tech industries rose 31%.

China's commerce ministry said foreign investment in high tech industries reached 252.8 billion yuan ($38 billion) in January to October, up 31% from a year earlier. Total foreign investment fell 2.4% to 606.9 billion yuan.

For you: Foreign companies are still building China tech supply chains. Expect more Chinese made electronics and parts in what you buy.

For you: Own an international fund? Check how much India it holds. Markets outside US tech are moving differently this month.

Europe

Russian missiles land in Poland during an attack on Ukraine.

Poland's defence minister said a Kh-101 cruise missile fell near Nowy Lipowiec, about 60 km from the Ukrainian border, a second missile at Rudnik nad Sanem and a likely drone at Mołodycz. No one was hurt. NATO said it stands in solidarity with Poland. The STOXX 600 fell 2.00%.

For you: Traveling in eastern Poland? Check government alerts. Defence spending in Europe is likely to keep rising.

Covers European Union, United Kingdom, Ukraine, Russia. Notes From Poland, Kyiv Independent, The Edge

Middle East

Iran claims it shot down a US drone over Hormuz.

Iran's Revolutionary Guards said on Saturday a new air defence system downed a US MQ-9 over the Strait of Hormuz, which they called the 58th such drone. The claim was carried by Iranian state media and is not independently confirmed.

For you: Any clash near Hormuz can move oil quickly. Watch fuel prices before you lock in holiday travel.

Covers Gulf states, Iran, Israel, Iraq, Yemen. Al Jazeera

Africa

South Africa holds rates at 7.25% in a split vote.

The South African Reserve Bank held its repo rate at 7.25% on Thursday by a 4 to 3 vote, with three members wanting a quarter point hike. October inflation rose to 5.2% from 4.5%. The next decision is January 28, 2027.

For you: Borrowing in rand? A close vote means a hike in January is a real possibility.

Covers Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Egypt. The Rio Times, Africa

South America

Argentina's peso hits 1,580 per dollar, its weakest in a year.

The peso closed at 1,580 per dollar on Friday, its weakest close in at least 12 months. In Brazil the real closed near 5.93 on Thursday, its weakest close since January 2025, even after a reported $1 billion central bank dollar sale.

For you: A trip to Argentina or Brazil is getting cheaper in dollars. Families there pay more for imports.

Fusion energy

Pacific Fusion gets a first of its kind license in New Mexico.

Pacific Fusion received New Mexico's first radioactive materials license for a private fusion company, covering tritium at its $1 billion Albuquerque campus. It aims for net facility gain by 2030. In Japan, Helical Fusion won up to ¥19.2 billion ($126 million) in government support.

For you: Fusion is still years from your power bill, but licenses like this are real steps. Be wary of anyone selling fusion stocks as a sure thing.

Batteries

A rechargeable battery that uses vitamin B12.

University of Cordoba researchers built a zinc air battery that uses vitamin B12 as its catalyst instead of costly metals. The prototype lasted up to 250 charge cycles.

For you: Cheaper, safer battery materials could lower costs for home storage over time. This is still lab work.
