Interest rates

The Fed is about to raise rates again. Your loans will feel it before your savings do.

High stakes for borrowers

Happened

October prices rose 0.4%, more than forecast. Core inflation printed 0.3% for a third straight month. Producer prices are up 6.5% over the year, the most since 2022.

Happening

The Fed's rate sits at 3.75% to 4.00%. The 10 year Treasury yield, which steers mortgages, is near 5.15%. Fed Chair Warsh speaks today at 11 a.m. Central.

Could happen

Quarter point hike Dec 991%
Half point hike9%

Market odds from CME FedWatch, November 10. Not a FIOPO forecast.

What it means for you

  • Credit cards and HELOCs track the Fed almost one for one. Pay down variable balances first.
  • Buying a home or refinancing? Rates are unlikely to fall before spring. Lock if a deal works today.
  • Savers win slowly. Move idle cash to an account that pays near the Fed rate, not 0.5%.

Iran war and the Gulf

Oil keeps flowing through Hormuz, but the backup route is now under fire.

High stakes for prices

Happened

Happening

Three US carriers are in the region. CENTCOM says 1.7 billion barrels have moved through Hormuz under escort. Qatar's LNG exports are running at about half of prewar levels.

AI and your retirement account

Nvidia lost its founder. Tuesday's earnings will test the stock behind a big slice of most index funds.

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Jensen Huang left as CEO after an internal probe. Chip stocks fell for two days, and the Nasdaq lost 0.9% on Friday.

Happening

Nasdaq futures are down about 0.8% this morning. South Korea's Kospi fell 3.4%, led by chipmakers.

Could happen

The report lands Tuesday after the close. A clear succession answer and steady guidance would likely spark a rebound. A messy answer could drag tech lower into December.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • If you own an S&P 500 or Nasdaq fund, you own a lot of Nvidia. A swing of 6% or more is normal on earnings day.

Trade and diplomacy

Trump, Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen Thursday. Don't expect a deal; do expect headlines.

Medium stakes for prices

Happened

Russia and Ukraine held their first talks in nine months and agreed a large prisoner swap. China ran its biggest naval drill yet around Taiwan.

Happening

APEC leaders meet November 18 and 19. A US official called the three way meeting a first conversation, not a deal. The US and China trade truce runs to January 10, 2027.

Could happen

Most likely: the truce is extended and chips stay unresolved. The risk: the truce lapses, and tariffs return on electronics, toys and clothing.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • Big purchases made in China, like phones, laptops and appliances, are safer to buy before January than after.
  • A Ukraine deal would be the biggest relief for fuel prices in 2027.
