Thanksgiving week

Gas is $3.64 as 81.5 million people get ready to travel for Thanksgiving.

Medium stakes for households

Happened

AAA's national average is $3.64 today, down from $3.82 a week ago and $4.27 a month ago, but still above $3.08 a year ago. AAA expects 81.5 million people to travel at least 50 miles from November 24 to 30, about 72.9 million by car. Average round trip domestic airfare is $800, up 17%.

Happening

US stock markets close on Thursday, November 26, and close early at 1 p.m. ET on Friday. Wednesday brings the October PCE inflation report, a second estimate of third quarter GDP, durable goods orders and jobless claims, moved up a day for the holiday. As of November 20, CME FedWatch market odds put a 99.3% chance on a quarter point Fed hike on December 9.

Could happen

Cheaper gas helps holiday budgets, but a hot inflation report on Wednesday would firm up the case for a December hike and higher card rates.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • Driving? Fill up early in the week, before any oil jump from the weekend's Hormuz attacks reaches stations.
  • Flying? Check your airline app the night before and pack for delays at busy hubs.
  • Carrying a card balance? Pay down what you can before the Fed meets on December 9.

Iran war and oil

Tankers are hit and Bahrain is attacked as Iran says the US blockade must be broken.

High stakes for prices

Happened

On Saturday UKMTO reported two tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's navy said a tanker in a restricted zone struck a mine. Bahrain said it intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks, and its interior ministry said a civilian industrial facility caught fire, with no casualties. Iran also claimed it hit a US F-16 and downed a US drone, claims not independently confirmed.

Happening

Iran is reportedly preparing 14 tankers to breach the US blockade in place for more than four months. A Qatari delegation held two hours of talks in Tehran on Friday. Iran says it will discuss Hormuz only once its demands are met, including an end to the war and lifted oil sanctions. President Trump told Fox News on Saturday he will make a deal "that is going to be permanent."

Nvidia

Nvidia had its worst week since October 2008, losing about $1 trillion in value.

High stakes for investors

Happened

Nvidia fell 23% last week and 14.01% on Friday alone to $125.53, after Reuters reported SEC staff are examining its accounting. The SEC has announced no enforcement action. Nvidia's forward price to earnings ratio has fallen to about 8, according to The Motley Fool.

Happening

Interim CEO Colette Kress said on the earnings call that Nvidia will be "far more selective" about AI industry financing deals. The chip sell-off spread: the VanEck Semiconductor ETF is down 40.5% from its June high and Oracle 68.0% from its peak. Dell and HP report on Tuesday, testing AI server demand.

Could happen

Until the SEC review and the CEO search are clearer, expect big swings in Nvidia and in the index funds that hold it.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact. Not investment advice.

What it means for you

  • Check how much Nvidia you own through S&P 500 and tech funds before deciding anything.
  • Do not sell in a panic. Rebalance only if tech has grown past the share you planned.
  • Trading single chip stocks this week? Use limit orders. Thin holiday trading can widen price gaps.

Stocks and your 401(k)

The S&P 500 lost 8.61% last week and is the most oversold since April 2025.

Medium stakes for savers

Happened

The S&P 500 ended Friday at 6,910.39 after seven straight losses, down 8.61% for the week but still up 0.76% for 2026. The Nasdaq lost 10.02% and is down 2.66% for the year. The S&P 500's relative strength reading fell below 30, the most oversold since April 2025, and the put to call ratio hit 1.14, the highest since then.

Happening

Only 43.2% of S&P 500 stocks are above their 50 day average, down from 71.8% a week earlier. Gold fell 8.92% on the week to $3,537 and Bitcoin was near $60,361 on Saturday. Treasuries held up, with the 10 year yield at 4.92%.

Could happen

Readings this oversold have often come before a bounce, but they do not mark a bottom on their own. A short holiday week can bring big moves on light volume.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact. Not investment advice.

What it means for you

  • Contributing to a 401(k)? Regular buys now purchase more shares at lower prices.
  • Need cash within a year? Keep it in savings or Treasuries, not stocks.
  • Rebalance on a schedule, not after a scary week.

Space

NASA fires a Moon rocket engine for a full 200 seconds.

On November 18 NASA ran developmental RS-25 engine No. 10001 for its full three minutes and 20 seconds at Stennis Space Center in Mississippi. Four RS-25s power each SLS rocket, with up to 2 million pounds of thrust. Artemis IV is planned for 2028.

For you: Space work supports jobs across Gulf Coast states. Watch for contractor hiring if you work in engineering.

ScienceDaily, NASA

Batteries

A pinch of manganese could cut copper from EV batteries.

Tohoku University and UACJ researchers added 1 atomic percent manganese to aluminum foil to make stable anodes, which could remove copper current collectors and coating steps. They say cycle life still needs to improve to match EV and grid needs.

For you: Cheaper materials could lower battery costs over time. This is still lab work, so do not delay an EV decision for it.

Nvidia will be more selective about funding its own customers.

Interim CEO Colette Kress said Nvidia will be "far more selective" about AI industry financing deals. Analysts are comparing this kind of vendor financing to Cisco's in the late 1990s, a view, not a finding.
