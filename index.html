Interest rates

Gas fell to $3.75, but borrowing costs are still headed up. The Fed minutes land today.

High stakes for borrowers

Happened

The AAA national average fell to $3.75 a gallon on November 18, from $4.30 a month ago. The 10 year Treasury yield, which steers mortgage rates, closed Tuesday at 5.11%.

Happening

Minutes from the Fed's October meeting come out at 2 p.m. Eastern today. New York Fed President John Williams said rates will likely need to rise again, but he opposed an aggressive path. October housing starts and building permits are also due.

Could happen

Markets almost fully price a quarter point Fed increase on December 9. The minutes will show how many officials wanted more.

Market pricing per Reuters, November 18. Not a FIOPO forecast.

  • Put the gas savings to work: move idle cash into an account paying close to the Fed rate.

AI and your retirement account

Nvidia's first report since Jensen Huang's exit beat estimates. Anthropic prices its IPO tonight.

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue, up 103%, and guided to $130 billion next quarter versus $125.25 billion expected. It returned $28.1 billion to shareholders in the quarter. The stock closed Tuesday at $155.46.

Happening

Nvidia rose 3.7% before the open, with chip and AI cloud stocks up more. The four biggest AI spenders, Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft and Meta, rose under 1%. Anthropic prices its IPO tonight, reportedly about six times oversubscribed, and trades Thursday as ANT.

Could happen

A strong guide eases fears that AI spending is peaking, but stocks fell for four days before this on rate and chip export worries. Expect big swings either way this week.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • If you own an S&P 500 or Nasdaq fund, you already own a lot of Nvidia. Earnings day swings are normal, so avoid trading on the headline.
  • Check that your retirement plan is not more concentrated in a few tech names than you intended.
  • First day IPO prices can swing hard. Size any Anthropic purchase as money you can afford to see drop.

Europe

UK inflation rose to 3.6% in October, pushed up by fuel.

Prices rose from 3.5% in September, the ONS said Wednesday. The Bank of England raised its rate to 4.00% on November 4 and meets again December 17. About 2.3 million UK households come off fixed rate mortgages next year.

For you: Paying a UK mortgage or sending pounds abroad? Another hike would raise loan costs and could support sterling.

Africa

Nigeria's inflation eased to 14.06% in October.

That is down from 14.63% in September, with food inflation at 19.13%. The central bank decides on rates November 24. South Africa reports October inflation today, forecast at 5.1%.

For you: Sending money to Nigeria? Slower inflation means each naira holds its value a little better. Compare transfer fees, which vary widely.

Covers Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, DR Congo, Ethiopia, Egypt. The Rio Times

South America

Brazil's Senate passed Lula's budget fix, 45 to 31.

The bill caps real spending growth at 2% in tight years from 2027 and trims corporate tax breaks. It could save R$45.1 to 56.1 billion and now goes to the lower house. The dollar buys about 5.77 reais.

For you: Dollars still stretch further in Brazil than at any time in the past year. Good for travelers and buyers of Brazilian goods.

Covers Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru. The Rio Times
