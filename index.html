Happening

AI and your retirement account

Nvidia reports tonight and Anthropic lists Thursday. One week will set the mood for AI stocks.

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Nvidia closed Monday at $156.14, down 4.1%, after its CEO's sudden exit and new US limits on chip access through overseas data centers. The Nasdaq fell 2.5%.

Happening

Nvidia reports after the close today under interim CEO Colette Kress. Anthropic set its IPO at $70 to $78 a share, up to a $1.404 trillion valuation, with trading expected Thursday under ANT on the Nasdaq.

Could happen

A steady Nvidia outlook and a calm Anthropic debut would likely lift tech into December. A weak guide could make the IPO price at the low end and extend the slide.

FIOPO read. Judgment, not fact.

What it means for you

  • If you own an S&P 500 or Nasdaq fund, you already own a lot of Nvidia. Big earnings day swings are normal.
  • Up to 20% of Anthropic's shares are set aside for retail investors. First day prices can swing hard, so size any purchase as money you can afford to see drop.
  • Check that your retirement plan is not more than you intended in a handful of tech names.

Sports and culture, followed by the money

The games and shows you love are now some of the biggest businesses on earth, and they set what you pay to watch and play.

Sports business

Baseball's labor deal expires December 1. A lockout looks likely, and the fight is over a salary cap.

Happened

MLB proposed delaying a salary cap to 2028, at $258.2 million with a $180.2 million floor. Players oppose a cap. The last cap fight led to the 1994 strike.

Happening

Teams are expected to rush free agent signings before the deal expires. Talks are unlikely to speed up until January.

Could happen

If 60 or more games are lost, national TV deals extend a year. A spring delay is possible. FIOPO read.

What it means for you

  • Hold off on 2027 season tickets and travel packages until a deal is signed, or buy only with a clear refund policy.
