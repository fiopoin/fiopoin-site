Happening

Medium stakes for investors

Happened

Happening

India and South Asia

India's inflation hit 6.09%, above the central bank's 6% ceiling.

October prices rose at the fastest pace in two years, driven by food. Food inflation reached 8.94%, with onions up 116%. The RBI raised its rate to 5.50% in October and meets again December 2 to 4.

For you: Sending money home or paying bills in rupees? Another RBI hike in December would raise loan costs there and could support the rupee.

Africa

The Ebola outbreak centered in DR Congo has passed 11,000 confirmed cases.

CDC counts 11,407 confirmed cases and 5,572 deaths as of November 14, almost all in DR Congo, with cases in Uganda, Kenya, Nigeria and France. This strain has no approved vaccine. CDC says the risk to the American public remains low.

For you: Travelers who were in DR Congo in the past 21 days cannot board flights to the US. Check CDC travel notices before any trip to the region.

Covers DR Congo, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Ethiopia, Egypt. CDC, WHO

South America

Brazil's real hit a 12 month low as Petrobras slid.

The dollar closed Friday at 5.67 reais, the most in a year. Brazil's central bank holds its rate at 13.75% and decides again December 9. Lula won reelection on October 25.

For you: Dollars stretch further in Brazil right now. Travelers and buyers of Brazilian goods benefit; people sending money to Brazil get more reais.

Covers Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru. The Rio Times, MercoPress
