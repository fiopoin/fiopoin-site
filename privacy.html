Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026.

FIOPO Intelligence (fiopoin.com) is free to read. You do not need an account, and we do not sell, rent or share personal information.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which stories are useful. It records the pages you view, the site that sent you, your general location (city or region, not your address), and your device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies to tell new visitors from returning ones. We see totals and trends, not who you are, and we do not combine this with other data about you.

Google processes this data under its own policy: how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block Google Analytics on every site with Google's opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking cookies in your browser. The site works the same either way.

Email

If you write to us at hello@fiopoin.com, we use your message and address only to reply. Email signup is not open yet; when it opens, we will use your address only to send the brief, and every email will have a one click unsubscribe.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security. See GitHub's note on data collection.

Questions

Write to hello@fiopoin.com and we will answer.
