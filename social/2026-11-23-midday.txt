Nvidia's board voted unanimously on Monday to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, CEO and board director, effective immediately, 11 days after he resigned. The board said input from senior US government officials gave it confidence his return poses no national security risk. Its independent investigation continues, and a new board committee will oversee export control and security risks. Before the news, Nvidia was up 8.69% in premarket trading.
