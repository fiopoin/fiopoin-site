CrowdStrike agreed on Monday to buy Zscaler in an all-stock deal valued at about $43.9 billion. Zscaler holders get 1.135 CrowdStrike shares each, about $260.41, a 34% premium. Founder Jay Chaudhry will join CrowdStrike's board. The deal needs shareholder and regulatory approval. General information, not financial advice. More at fiopoin.com
