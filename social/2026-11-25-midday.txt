Sanofi agreed to buy Insmed for $175 a share in cash, about $41 billion. The deal adds Brinsupri, a once daily lung disease pill that Sanofi says earned $1.09 billion through September. It is expected to close in the first half of 2027 and is funded with debt and cash. General information, not financial advice. More at fiopoin.com
