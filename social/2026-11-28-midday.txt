Dangote Petroleum Refinery is set to begin trading on the Nigerian Exchange on Tuesday, December 1, after a share sale that raised $2.08 billion, 30% above its base target, Billionaires.Africa reports citing Bloomberg. The listing values the refinery at about $47.8 billion.
